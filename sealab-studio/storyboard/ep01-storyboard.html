Three ways to cut Episode 1

All three use the same script, the same timings, the same data, and the same art rules. They differ in where the camera spends its time. Estimated runtime for each is about 65 seconds (1,555 frames at 24 fps) until your real recording sets the exact length.

Frame by frame

Shot board

Timecodes are estimates at about 2.6 spoken words per second. Your recording replaces them: shots snap to the actual word timestamps, so the plan stays the same and only the numbers move.

Before I build

Questions for you

1 · Which option?A, B or C, or a mix (for example B's gym scenes with C's split-screen results). I recommend B: it uses the staging that already worked in your proof of concept.
2 · Whose mouth moves?You voice every line. Should only Marc lip-flap (Lena stands centre for the caveat but listens), or should the mouth flap go to whichever character owns the beat, so Lena "speaks" the caveat in your voice?
3 · Who sits on the leg extension?The study used untrained women. None of the five cast members fits: Kai is a 19-year-old athlete and Margaret is 68. I can add a sixth recurring client in a muted colour, or use Margaret, or use Kai.
4 · The 2-second end cardThe brief says don't speak the teaser, and also that video length must match the audio within 0.5 s. I'll add 2 s of silence (or room tone) after your last word unless you'd rather record the "Next:" line yourself.
5 · Series nameThe end card has a small wordmark. Give me a name or I'll leave a placeholder.

Things I found in the brief and how I'll handle them

Five groups, four-bar limitEpisode 1 has five groups but CompareBars allows four. I show the three range groups as bars, then add "Alternating" and "No training" as a second card on the words that name them.
Strength has no numbersThe data says who won on strength, not by how much. I show it as labelled chips, not bars, so no bar length implies a figure we don't have.
Numbers said as words"Three of the four" and "two of those" are numbers. The validator will read number words, and the data block gets regionsMeasured: 4, bentWinsRegions: 3, otherApproxNoneRegions: 2.
